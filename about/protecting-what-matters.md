# What does Sakshi do?
## Protecting What Matters

If you've read [how this whole idea started](the-origin-story.md), you know that my main motivation was stopping crucial evidence from being wiped out by corrupt officials or anyone abusing their power.

But as I was building Sākṣī, I realized the philosophy goes way beyond just protecting evidence.

It boils down to this:

> **Keep what matters safe. Keep what is private private. Preserve what you cannot afford to lose.**

## Beating Forced Deletion

Our phones aren't built for situations where someone is literally standing over you, demanding you delete a file. If you say no, things can get physical. So, you comply. You delete it.

Sākṣī changes that dynamic. By saving a copy to the hidden [Vault](../overview/README.md#saksi-vault), you can go ahead and delete the visible video from your gallery once the complete copy is in the Vault. The person threatening you thinks they won't be exposed, but your evidence is still safe.

And because I know things can get chaotic—like your phone dying or getting snatched while you are still recording—I made sure Sākṣī continuously saves fragments of the video *while* it's recording if you use Fragmented MP4 (fMP4). If you use Normal MP4, it is copied only after recording stops. Even if the recording stops abruptly with fMP4, whatever was captured is already in the Vault.

## It's Also About Personal Privacy

While protecting evidence is huge, I realized this system is perfect for normal, everyday privacy too.

We all have personal photos, videos, or documents we just don't want anyone else casually swiping through. Most people use "locked folders," but let's be honest, locked folders are obvious. If a friend or family member sees a locked folder, they are going to ask you to open it.

With Sākṣī, you can use the [Portal](../overview/README.md#saksi-portal) to drop your private files into the Vault, and then just uninstall the Portal.

Now, there is no locked folder icon. There's no Portal app. There is just your normal phone. The Vault is still there in the background holding your files. While it remains visible in Android's installed-app list in Settings, nobody knows to look for it. When you want to see your files again, you just reinstall the Portal. Out of sight, out of mind.

## What Sākṣī Is (and Isn't)

I want to be completely straight with you. Sākṣī is designed to hide your files from casual snooping and protect you from forced deletion in the heat of the moment.

But I'm not claiming it's magic.
* If a hardcore tech expert gets ahold of your phone and starts digging through system files, they might find the Vault.
* It doesn't upload your files to the cloud. If your phone gets completely destroyed, the data is gone.

If you want the super technical details on what Sākṣī protects against, read the [Security & Privacy Overview](../security-privacy/README.md).

At the end of the day, I built Sākṣī to give you a fighting chance to hold onto the truth and keep your private life private.
