# Scheduling Deletions and Recovery

Sākṣī Vault does not delete files immediately when you request it. Instead, it uses a delayed deletion system to protect against accidental or forced removal.

When you delete a file from the Vault using the Portal, it is **scheduled for deletion** and hidden from the main gallery. The Vault will permanently erase it after 30 days.

## How to Schedule a Deletion

1. Open the **Sākṣī Portal** to view the main gallery.
2. Select file(s) to delete:
   - **Viewer mode (Single File):** Tap a photo or video to view it. Tap the **Schedule deletion** icon (a trash can with a clock) in the top right corner. A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap **Schedule deletion** to confirm.
   - **Selection mode (Multiple Files):** **Press and hold** any item to enter selection mode, then tap additional items or use the **Select All** icon. Tap the **Schedule deletion** button on the bottom action bar. Multiple deletions happen immediately upon tapping the button.
3. The selected file(s) are immediately scheduled for deletion and removed from the main gallery.

*Note: This action only affects the copy inside the Vault. Any copies you previously exported/saved to your device, or the original files taken by your phone's camera, are not affected.*

## Viewing and Recovering Scheduled Deletions

If you change your mind within the 30-day window, you can recover the file.

1. On the main gallery screen, tap the **More Options** (three vertical dots) menu.
2. Tap **Recover Media** (or "Scheduled Deletions").
3. The gallery will now display only the files that are currently scheduled for deletion.
4. Select files to recover:
   - **Single File:** Tap the file you want to recover to open the viewer.
   - **Multiple Files:** **Press and hold** any item to enter selection mode, then tap additional items or use the **Select All** icon.
5. Tap the **Recover** icon (a clock with an arrow) in the top right corner (or top app bar in selection mode).
6. For single recovery, a confirmation dialog will appear. Tap **Recover**. Multiple recoveries happen immediately.
7. The selected file(s) are restored to the normal gallery, and their scheduled deletion is canceled.

To return to the normal gallery, tap the back arrow in the top left corner of the Scheduled Deletions screen.
